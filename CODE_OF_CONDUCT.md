# Community Conduct

Please Continue is in early development. Its community spaces and formal
governance processes are still taking shape.

Treat other people with respect. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal
attacks, unwanted disclosure of private information, and other conduct that
makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

Formal reporting and enforcement procedures have not yet been published. Do
not place private or sensitive information in a public repository message.

This file will be revised as the project's community governance matures.
